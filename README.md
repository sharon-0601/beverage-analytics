# 📊 Beverage Price & Product Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes beverage product data collected from **Pforzheim, Karlsruhe, and Stuttgart, Germany**.

The objective is to clean, standardize, combine, and analyze product datasets to identify **pricing patterns, product distributions, package-size differences, deposit (Pfand) relationships, and city-level variations**.

The project demonstrates a practical **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** workflow using Python and Pandas.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Clean and standardize product datasets from multiple sources
* Combine datasets into a single analysis-ready dataset
* Analyze product and category distributions
* Compare average prices across categories
* Identify the cheapest and most expensive products
* Calculate and compare **price per liter**
* Analyze **Pfand (deposit)** relative to product price
* Compare alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverage pricing
* Analyze product price distributions
* Compare products and categories across cities
* Create visualizations to communicate findings

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project contains beverage product information from:

* 📍 Pforzheim
* 📍 Karlsruhe

* Product name

* Price
* Package size
* Price per liter
* Pfand
* Alcoholic / non-alcoholic classification
* City / location

The datasets were cleaned and standardized before being combined for analysis.

## 📊 Key Analysis

### 1. Product & Category Distribution

Analyzed the number and distribution of products across different beverage categories.

### 2. Average Price by Category

Calculated average product prices to identify pricing differences between beverage categories.

### 3. Cheapest & Most Expensive Products

Identified products with the lowest and highest prices within the dataset.

### 4. Price per Liter

Used price-per-liter calculations to make products with different package sizes more comparable.

### 5. Pfand Analysis

Analyzed the relationship between beverage prices and **Pfand (deposit)** amounts.

### 6. Alcoholic vs. Non-Alcoholic Products

Compared pricing patterns between alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages.

### 7. Price Distribution

Visualized the distribution of product prices to understand common price ranges and potential outliers.

### 8. City Comparison

Compared product categories and pricing patterns across **Pforzheim, Karlsruhe, and Stuttgart**.
